# Survival (day/night, meters, campfire, downed)
Tier: standard
Purpose: Server-authoritative day/night clock, per-player health/warmth/hunger, campfires with fuel, and downed/revive.
Spec: docs/specs/run-loop.md, docs/specs/survival.md.
Owner files: src/server/Survival/{DayNightService,SurvivalService,FireService}.luau, src/shared/logic/{DayClock,FireLogic,SurvivalLogic}.luau, tests/SurvivalTests.luau
Public API: DayNightService.getDay/isNight/getElapsed/setDay/setClockSpeed. SurvivalService.getMeters/isDowned/damage/heal/feed/warm. FireService.isWarm(Vector3)/isLit/addFuel/getLitFires()/getTier/handleUpgrade; Remote UpgradeFireRequest(fire) -> type, registered, Guard (Fire.UpgradeRateInterval), downed, Fire.UpgradeRange, tier cap, cost from Config.Fire.Tiers (server recomputes; items removed atomically after count check), applyTier sets Tier/WarmRadius/BuildRadius/LightRange attributes. Client ring: src/client/Fire/RadiusRing.luau (attribute-driven dots, capped by Fire.Ring). setFuelSpender(fn(player, itemId, amount) -> boolean); fuel is data-driven: any item with Config.Items.Entries[id].fuelSeconds (tier-1 burn seconds; scaled by the tier WoodFuelSeconds ratio; Wood 120, Stick 40, Pinecone 30, Blubber 300), picked by FireLogic.pickFuel (selected item, else Config.Fire.FuelOrder). Remotes: MetersUpdate (server -> client, MetersPayload); AddFuelRequest (fire: Instance, itemId: string?); ReviveRequest (target: Player). Debug handlers: setDay, clockSpeed, invincible.
Data touched: Workspace attributes Day/IsNight/ClockHour (Config.Time); Player attributes Warmth, Hunger, Downed (Config.Downed.StateAttribute); Campfire attributes FuelSeconds, Lit; CollectionService tag Config.Fire.Tag. Health mirrors to Humanoid.Health. Nothing persisted.
Invariants: meters stay within [0, Max]; day counter never decreases except via debug setDay; warmth drains outside a lit fire radius (x night multiplier at night) and regenerates inside; downed players' meters are frozen and bleed out after BleedoutSeconds; a player with no able teammate dies instead of going down; fuel is within [0, MaxFuelSeconds]; same seed gives the same 100-day sim result.
Exploit surface: AddFuelRequest and ReviveRequest are type-checked, rate limited (Guard, Net.UseRateInterval), state-checked (alive, not downed) and range-checked on the server; wood is spent through a server-only spender hook; the revive channel is re-validated each tick; the client never sends meter values.
Playtest checklist: 1. Local player stands outside a fire at night, warmth falls; inside the radius it rises. 2. Add wood near a campfire, FuelSeconds rises; far away it is rejected. 3. DebugService setDay/clockSpeed/invincible change the clock and block damage. 4. Two players: one downed, the other revives within ReviveSeconds. Script: tools/qa (survival scenario, not yet written).
Status: in progress
Notes: outside damage dealt directly to the Humanoid is absorbed as health loss (Dead state is disabled; the service kills explicitly). Clothing tier, bed/sleeping bag and blizzards are not wired yet.

## Integration points
- Exposes: FireService.register(fire) (placement), count() (fire cap), getLitPositions() (mobs repel edge), isLit, isWarm, addFuel, handleAddFuel (QA/server path, full validation). Attribute names Config.Fire.FuelAttribute/LitAttribute, Config.Meters.WarmthAttribute/HungerAttribute.
- Consumes: wood spender wired in init.server to InventoryService.remove(player, itemId, n); camp campfire built by ChunkService; placed campfires from PlacementService; DayNightService debug handlers (setDay, clockSpeed).

## Fire meter and ring (round 3)
- Server: FireService publishes attribute Config.Fire.FuelFractionAttribute (FireLogic.fraction(fuel, tier max), 0.01 steps) on register, add fuel, upgrade, going out, and while burning at most every Config.Fire.Meter.UpdateInterval (inside the existing burn loop). Works for every tagged fire (upgraded, extra).
- Client: src/client/Fire/FireMeter.luau, one RenderStepped loop, a BillboardGui per tagged fire (PlayerGui, name Meter.GuiName), shown within Meter.ShowRange, colour FullColor -> LowColor, pulses at or under Meter.LowFraction; cleaned on fire Destroying/untag.
- RadiusRing: day colours (WarmColorDay/BuildColorDay, cool and darker than snow) vs night glow; day transparency must stay <= Ring.DayVisibilityMaxTransparency (tests + placement-preview assert it).
